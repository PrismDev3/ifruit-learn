iFruit OSPUBLIC BUILD LINKS

Build files and launch links.

iFruit OS is a browser-based desktop simulation inspired by the iFruit universe. This page lists its published demo files, CDN copies, and hosting options.

The standalone files bundle the app shell. The complete local game and media asset library is not included in this small public build, so some previews and games may need their original hosts or a full asset deployment.
